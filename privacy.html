Count & Canvas

Privacy Policy

Effective August 14, 2026

Count & Canvas is built to respect your privacy completely. The short version: we don't collect anything. The app has no account, no sign-up, and no analytics, and it does not send your information anywhere.

Information we collect

None. Count & Canvas does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no user accounts and no login.

Where your work is stored

Your patterns, projects, floss stash, and settings are saved locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or to anyone else.

Photos

The "photo to pattern" feature works only on a photo you choose. The image is processed entirely on your device to generate a stitch chart. Photos are never uploaded, shared, or stored by us. If you decline photo access, every other feature still works.

Network use

Count & Canvas is designed to work fully offline. It does not include advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs, and it makes no calls to our servers (we don't operate any).

Sharing and exporting

When you choose to export or print a pattern, the file is handed to the standard iOS share sheet or print dialog. What happens next — saving to Files, AirDrop, email, printing — is controlled entirely by you through Apple's system features.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email countandcanvas.app@gmail.com.
